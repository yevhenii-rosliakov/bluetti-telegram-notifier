Sign in to BLUETTI (once; tokens are stored on the volume and picked up within ~5 min):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it deploy/{{ include "bgb.fullname" . }} -- bluetti-grid-bot login

your Secret so a fresh volume can be seeded from it.
